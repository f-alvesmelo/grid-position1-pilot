# Concepts

Command: `/theory`. Sources: [[Project]], [[Measurement Research Design]], [[Refined Measurement Framework]]. RESEARCHER MATERIAL.

## Political Alignment

Multilevel configuration of political relationships among three actors: national government, local authority, and displaced population. Not a single scalar; assessed dyad-by-dyad. See [[Political Alignment]].

## Institutional Location of Authority (`authority_locus`)

Where formal/effective authority over a given policy domain sits: **National (N) / Local (L) / Shared (S) / Delegated (D) / Unclear (U)** — see [[Refined Measurement Framework]] §4. This is the conditioning factor in H3 — it determines *whose* alignment matters most for a given domain's response.

✓ **Refined 2026-09-27**: `authority_locus` is explicitly **not** a country-level characteristic — it is coded per **domain and period/episode**, since the same state can have national authority over one domain (e.g., legal status) and delegated or local authority over another (e.g., humanitarian assistance implementation). It is derived from four separately-recorded indicators — formal legal allocation, budgetary authority, administrative/implementation authority, and observed decision-making — rather than assessed impressionistically. See [[Data Dictionary]] and [[Coding Protocol]] for the derivation procedure.

## Government Response

The outcome of interest: a **multidimensional profile** of government behaviour toward displaced populations, decomposed by policy domain and, within each domain, by de jure vs. de facto response. See [[Protection]] and [[Repression]] for the two poles of the response classification.

## Policy Domain

The specific area of government activity being coded (e.g., humanitarian assistance, public services, rights and legal guarantees, mobility and territorial control). The pilot operationalizes **one domain only: humanitarian assistance** (see [[Scope & Limitations]]).

## De Jure Response

Formal laws, policies, regulations, decrees, institutional commitments, or eligibility provisions. Coded independently of what actually happens in practice.

## De Facto Response

Actual practices or actions by state actors — provision, facilitation, restriction, selective exclusion, withdrawal, delegation, or tolerated practices — that may or may not correspond to the de jure position.

## Displaced Population (as a political actor)

Not assumed to be politically homogeneous. Operationalized as the **specific, politically salient displaced population or group** tied to a given displacement episode, identified using evidence from the period immediately preceding or surrounding displacement onset (see [[Political Alignment]]).

## Displacement Episode

The implicit unit around which alignment is measured and to which a government response is attributed. Distinguishing state-caused displacement from displacement caused by other actors is listed as a methodological safeguard (see [[Measurement Research Design]] §9), though not yet built into a formal variable.
