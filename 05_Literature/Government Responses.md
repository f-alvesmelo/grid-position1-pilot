# Government Responses

Command: `/literature`. Sources: [[Project]]. See [[Literature Map]] for the full citation table.

## From Displacement Production to Displacement Response

Lichtenheld & Steele (2026) document that states adopt formal policies toward IDPs, but implementation varies across contexts and policy domains, and informal practice can diverge from formal commitment. This is the literature-level source of the project's de jure/de facto distinction, now fully operationalized in [[Coding Protocol]] and [[Data Dictionary]].

## The GRID Framework Itself

Steele, Schwartz, & Lichtenheld (2024) is the GRID project's own foundational paper: it conceptualizes government response along a **protection–repression continuum** and proposes that **political alignment among multi-level actors** shapes that response (see [[Core Argument]]). This project is explicitly positioned as an extension of that framework — disaggregating "alignment" into a multilevel configuration ([[Political Alignment]]) and "response" into a multi-domain, de jure/de facto structure ([[Data Dictionary]]).

## What This Body of Literature Does Not Yet Specify (per the project text)

- A measurement instrument for political alignment at the multilevel/configurational level — this project's own contribution (now operationalized via the two researcher memos, see [[Political Alignment]]).
- Domain-specific institutional authority as a moderator of the alignment–response relationship — this project's `authority_locus` construct (see [[Concepts]], [[Mechanisms]]).
