## Alignment Timing Rule (Applies Across All Tiers)

Regardless of the overall temporal scope of a tier, **political alignment must always be measured pre-displacement or at onset**, before the displacement episode and before the government response being explained (see [[Political Alignment]]). This is a within-episode temporal ordering rule, distinct from the tier's overall start/end-year coverage:

1. Pre-displacement or onset-period political alignment
2. Displacement episode
3. Subsequent government response

## Institutional (Non-Substantive) Timeline

The four-year PhD timeline (GRID, University of Amsterdam, 2026–2031; see [[Project Brief]]) governs *when the researcher does the work*, not the temporal scope of the data being analyzed. The two should not be conflated: e.g., cross-national data coding may cover 1989–present even though the PhD project itself runs 2026–2031.
